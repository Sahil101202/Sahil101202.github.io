MYSTRAVYN Automation is an independent application used by the owner of the MYSTRAVYN YouTube channel to help manage original video publishing, verify the connected channel, and review channel performance through authorized YouTube APIs.

01 / Access

Channel verification

Connect to the intended channel only after Google OAuth consent and confirm its identity before any authorized action.

02 / Production

Original publishing

Support creation and authorized uploading of original documentaries and Shorts, with visibility settings under channel-owner control.

03 / Insights

Read-only analytics

Review YouTube performance data such as views, watch time and subscriber totals to understand how uploaded content performs.

About this application

MYSTRAVYN Automation is currently a limited-use tool intended for the MYSTRAVYN channel owner, not a consumer-facing service or public Google sign-in platform. It uses Google OAuth only to obtain permissions chosen by the authorizing account and does not ask for the account's Google password.

It uses YouTube Data API Services and YouTube Analytics API. To understand the data accessed and the available revocation and deletion options, read the Privacy Policy.

Google and YouTube services are subject to their own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
